# 阈值出处：文献书目

> **每一个阈值都要能说出出处。** 审稿人问"为什么是 .50 不是 .40"，你要答得出来。

---

## 一、SEM 通用教科书

- **Kline, R. B. (2016).** *Principles and Practice of Structural Equation Modeling* (4th ed.). Guilford Press.
  > **SEM 必备**。偏度 < 3 / 峰度 < 8 的常引出处；拟合指标、模型识别、报告规范的标准参考。

- **Byrne, B. M. (2016).** *Structural Equation Modeling with AMOS* (3rd ed.). Routledge.
  > **AMOS 专用教程**。测量不变性、多群组分析、模型修正的操作与解释，写得最清楚。

- **Hair, J. F., Black, W. C., Babin, B. J., & Anderson, R. E. (2019).** *Multivariate Data Analysis* (8th ed.). Cengage.
  > 载荷 ≥ .50、CR ≥ .70、AVE ≥ .50 的常引出处；样本量建议。

---

## 二、拟合指标

- **Hu, L., & Bentler, P. M. (1999).** Cutoff criteria for fit indexes in covariance structure analysis. *Structural Equation Modeling, 6*(1), 1–55.
  > **最重要的一篇**。CFI ≥ .95、RMSEA ≤ .06、SRMR ≤ .08 的双指标准则就出自这里。引它准没错。

- **Browne, M. W., & Cudeck, R. (1993).** Alternative ways of assessing model fit. In *Testing Structural Equation Models*. Sage.
  > RMSEA ≤ .05（良好）/ ≤ .08（可接受）的出处。

- **Marsh, H. W., Hau, K.-T., & Wen, Z. (2004).** In search of golden rules. *Structural Equation Modeling, 11*(3), 320–341.
  > **对"金标准"的批判**。提醒不要机械套用 cutoff。答辩时引这篇可以为略低的拟合指标辩护。

---

## 三、信度与效度

- **Fornell, C., & Larcker, D. F. (1981).** Evaluating structural equation models with unobservable variables and measurement error. *Journal of Marketing Research, 18*(1), 39–50.
  > **AVE ≥ .50、CR ≥ .70、√AVE > 相关系数（区分效度）的原始出处**。引用率极高。

- **Henseler, J., Ringle, C. M., & Sarstedt, M. (2015).** A new criterion for assessing discriminant validity. *Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science, 43*(1), 115–135.
  > **HTMT 准则**（< .85 / < .90）。近年比 Fornell-Larcker 更被推荐，建议两个都报。

- **Nunnally, J. C., & Bernstein, I. H. (1994).** *Psychometric Theory* (3rd ed.). McGraw-Hill.
  > Cronbach's α ≥ .70 的经典出处。

---

## 四、共同方法偏差

- **Podsakoff, P. M., MacKenzie, S. B., Lee, J.-Y., & Podsakoff, N. P. (2003).** Common method biases in behavioral research. *Journal of Applied Psychology, 88*(5), 879–903.
  > **CMB 的权威文献**。Harman 单因子、CLF 潜在共同方法因子、事前程序控制都在这里。做自评问卷必引。

- **Podsakoff, P. M., et al. (2012).** Sources of method bias in social science research and recommendations. *Annual Review of Psychology, 63*, 539–569.
  > 上一篇的更新版。

---

## 五、样本量

- **Bentler, P. M., & Chou, C.-P. (1987).** Practical issues in structural modeling. *Sociological Methods & Research, 16*(1), 78–117.
  > **N : 参数 ≥ 5:1（理想 10:1）** 的出处。

- **Wolf, E. J., et al. (2013).** Sample size requirements for structural equation models. *Educational and Psychological Measurement, 73*(6), 913–934.
  > 用蒙特卡洛模拟给出不同模型的样本量需求，比"200 人"这种笼统说法有力得多。

---

## 六、中介与调节

- **Preacher, K. J., & Hayes, A. F. (2008).** Asymptotic and resampling strategies for assessing and comparing indirect effects in multiple mediator models. *Behavior Research Methods, 40*(3), 879–891.
  > **Bootstrap 检验中介效应的权威出处**。为什么不用 Sobel，这里讲得最清楚。

- **Zhao, X., Lynch, J. G., & Chen, Q. (2010).** Reconsidering Baron and Kenny. *Journal of Consumer Research, 37*(2), 197–206.
  > **对"完全中介/部分中介"提法的批判**。建议改用"间接效应显著"的表述。审稿人常引这篇。

- **Cheung, G. W., & Rensvold, R. B. (2002).** Evaluating goodness-of-fit indexes for testing measurement invariance. *Structural Equation Modeling, 9*(2), 233–255.
  > **ΔCFI < .01** 判定测量不变性的出处。多群组分析必引。

- **Hayes, A. F. (2022).** *Introduction to Mediation, Moderation, and Conditional Process Analysis* (3rd ed.). Guilford Press.
  > PROCESS 的圣经。显变量的中介调节用这个比 AMOS 方便得多。

---

## 七、缺失值

- **Little, R. J. A. (1988).** A test of missing completely at random for multivariate data with missing values. *JASA, 83*(404), 1198–1202.
  > **Little's MCAR 检验**的出处。

- **Enders, C. K. (2010).** *Applied Missing Data Analysis.* Guilford Press.
  > FIML 与多重插补的标准参考。

---

## 八、模型修正的诚信问题

- **MacCallum, R. C., Roznowski, M., & Necowitz, L. B. (1992).** Model modifications in covariance structure analysis: The problem of capitalization on chance. *Psychological Bulletin, 111*(3), 490–504.
  > **必读**。用 MI 反复修模型 = 利用样本的偶然性，结果无法复现。
  > **这篇是"不要滥用修正指数"最有力的依据**——也是审稿人质疑你时会引的那篇。

---

## 引用速查

| 你要报告的 | 引谁 |
|---|---|
| CFI ≥ .95, RMSEA ≤ .06, SRMR ≤ .08 | Hu & Bentler (1999) |
| 偏度 < 3, 峰度 < 8 | Kline (2016) |
| 载荷 ≥ .50, CR ≥ .70, AVE ≥ .50 | Fornell & Larcker (1981); Hair et al. (2019) |
| √AVE > 相关（区分效度） | Fornell & Larcker (1981) |
| HTMT < .85 | Henseler et al. (2015) |
| Harman 单因子 < 40% | Podsakoff et al. (2003) |
| N:p ≥ 5:1 | Bentler & Chou (1987) |
| Bootstrap 检验中介 | Preacher & Hayes (2008) |
| ΔCFI < .01（测量不变性） | Cheung & Rensvold (2002) |
| Little's MCAR | Little (1988) |
| 不滥用 MI | MacCallum et al. (1992) |
